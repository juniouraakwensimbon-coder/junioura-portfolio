Common mythsabout websites

Myths

  • Websites are only for big businesses.
  • A Facebook Page means you don’t need a website.
  • A website only needs to look good.
  • Websites don’t need maintenance after launch.
  • Everyone needs a website immediately.

The truth:

A website should serve a clear purpose, be useful, easy to navigate, responsive, and maintained over time.

Before building one, ask:“What do I want my website to achieve?”
